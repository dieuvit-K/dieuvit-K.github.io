04 · Research

In the lab

Biochemistry, applied microbiology and biotechnology.

PhD thesis Ongoing

Assessing the biotechnological potential of native rhizobacteria for the climate resilience of maize and legumes and for soil bioremediation in the Republic of the Congo

Supervisor: Prof. Aimé Christian KAYATH, Full Professor, Faculty of Science and Technology, Marien Ngouabi University

Affiliations: IRSEN (Cité scientifique) and IRA (Laboratory of bromatology and agri-food technologies)

  1. Isolation and characterisation of plant growth-promoting rhizobacteria (PGPR) adapted to Congolese soils
  2. Effect of the strains on growth and drought tolerance of maize and legumes
  3. Biochemical traits: phosphate solubilisation, nitrogen fixation, IAA, ACC deaminase and siderophores
  4. Soil bioremediation prospects for resilient agriculture

2025

International Journal of Microbiology · June 2025

High Efficacy of Rose Bengal in Reducing the Pathogenicity of Escherichia coli Isolated From Diarrheal Infections

Co-author · IRSEN, Brazzaville

Antibacterial activity of rose bengal, a photosensitising dye, against E. coli strains from diarrhoeal infections: significant inhibition under light activation, with prospects for antimicrobial photodynamic therapy.

DOI: 10.1155/ijm/4912438
