About Me

With a Master's degree in Integrative Physiology and Experimental Physiopathology from Université Marien Ngouabi and ongoing studies in Biology and Health at Université Paris-Saclay, I have built strong expertise in experimental neurobiology and behavioral studies.

My work at CNRS/IDEEV involved designing and executing a study on gustatory perception in honeybees, utilizing Pavlovian conditioning and fluorescence microscopy to contribute to a cellular and molecular neurobiology project.

My academic and research journey also includes comprehensive experience in experimental physiopathology and cardiovascular studies, with a focus on diabetes and hypertension models.

Actively seeking Ph.D. opportunities to further my contributions to advancing health through neuroscience and experimental physiology.
